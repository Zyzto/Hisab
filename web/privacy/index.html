Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Summary

Hisab is a local-only open-source application. The public application does not create accounts, send app data to a service, show ads, or collect usage analytics.

Local data

Groups, participants, expenses, balances, settlements, receipt images, scanner drafts, and settings are stored in the local SQLite database on your device. The app reads and writes this data to provide its features. It is not transmitted by the application.

Permissions

Camera and photo-library access are requested only when you choose to scan or attach a receipt. Android notification access is requested only when you enable the local transaction scanner. Captured text and drafts remain on the device.

Backups and deletion

You control local backups through Settings → Data & Backup. You can export, import, or delete local records at any time. Deleting app data through the operating system removes the remaining local database.

Third-party components

Hisab uses open-source Flutter, SQLite/PowerSync, and platform OCR components. The public app does not initialize an external data service. Platform stores and the browser may collect information under their own policies when you download or run an application.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published with a new application release. Questions can be raised through the project's public issue tracker.
