What Hisab does

Shared trips, household costs, and personal budgets — with balances that stay clear when everyone chips in. Works offline; optional cloud sync.

Open the web app

  • Groups & people

    Trips, events, or household lists — with participants linked to real accounts when online.

  • Expenses

    Multi-currency amounts, categories, receipts, equal / shares / exact-amount splits, and transfers.

  • Balance & settle-up

    Who owes whom, minimal settlement suggestions, and record payments in one tap.

  • Offline-first

    Full local storage. Sync, invites, and members when you attach a cloud backend.

  • English & Arabic

    The name Hisab comes from Arabic حساب — settling who owes whom. The app is fully RTL in Arabic.

  • Personal lists

    Solo budgets and spending without split UI — plus an optional Android notification scanner.
